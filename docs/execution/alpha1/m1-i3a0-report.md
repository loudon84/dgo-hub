## Concurrency regression

The original lock trigger took `FOR UPDATE` on the operation for every child-item and outbox update. In the real two-transaction test, transaction A updated the child item `RECEIVED` to `VALIDATING` and held its transaction open; transaction B updated only the outbox acknowledgment and hit `55P03` after its one-second lock timeout on that operation row. The exact RED is `E:\m1t5-runtime\f4af1d9e-d1a2-4a48-8f23-f3268f704ee6\state-concurrency-red.log`. The migration now takes sorted operation-row locks only before structural child-item/outbox insert, delete, or `job_id` reassignment; the final checker reads without taking that lock. State, lease and ACK changes do not take global/source/operation locks. The paired regression shows item status and ACK updates can proceed concurrently, while a concurrent structural outbox deletion and item insertion serialize. `E:\m1t5-runtime\58e70bd0-75ab-4525-89d9-3426a2bc09d9\schema-green.log` records that bounded GREEN; the final gate covers the expanded fixture.
